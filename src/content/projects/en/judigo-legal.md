summary: Legal platform with real-time inquiries, AI-suggested answers and instantly translated chat.
headline: Legal inquiries that reach the right lawyer, with chat translated in real time.
problem: People with legal questions didn't know which lawyer to ask, and lawyers wasted time sorting through inquiries outside their practice area. On top of that, client and lawyer didn't always speak the same language.
solution: I started on a two-person team with a senior engineer and later became the project lead, mentoring an intern. A client posts an inquiry and it shows up in real time only to lawyers in that practice area; those on the top plan also get an SMS and an email. While reviewing the details, OpenAI suggests a possible answer to the lawyer. Once the lawyer accepts, both move into a chat I built end to end on Firebase, with a counter and messages translated between English and Spanish in real time.
outcome: Every inquiry reaches only the right specialist, the lawyer starts from an AI-suggested answer, and the conversation happens with no language barrier.
