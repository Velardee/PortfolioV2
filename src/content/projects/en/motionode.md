summary: AI-powered project manager built as an Asana alternative.
headline: An AI project manager where everything happens in real time.
problem: Motionode set out to replace Asana with AI. To compete with a tool like that, collaboration had to feel instant and nobody could lose their place when closing the app.
solution: I worked across many parts of the product. The highlights were the real-time features, task notifications and comments over GraphQL and Apollo, on schemas and endpoints I designed with Node.js, Express, Sequelize and MariaDB. Then came session persistence, so if you closed the app inside a project, coming back took you straight to the last thing you were looking at. I built the frontend in React, Material UI and Redux Toolkit.
outcome: Real-time collaboration and automatic return to your last context, in production.
